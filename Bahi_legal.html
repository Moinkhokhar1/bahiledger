Section 01

Privacy Policy

In short: Bahi runs entirely on your device. Your bills, stock, shop and customer records are stored in a local database and are never uploaded to a server, because Bahi doesn't have one. We don't collect analytics, don't show ads, and don't ask you to create an account.

1. Who this applies to

This policy covers the Bahi mobile and desktop application ("Bahi", "the app"), built by Moin Khokhar. It explains what information the app handles and how, so you can decide whether it fits how you run your business.

2. What Bahi does not do

  • Bahi does not require you to sign up or log in with a name, phone number, or email.
  • Bahi does not send your bills, stock lists, or shop/customer data to any server — there is no server.
  • Bahi does not use analytics, tracking pixels, or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • Bahi does not show ads or share your information with advertisers.

3. The data you enter

Everything you type into Bahi — products, stock quantities, GST rates, bills, shop names, delivery records, and running balances — is saved locally on your device using an on-device database (SQLite). Only you and apps you explicitly grant access to (like your device's own backup tools) can reach that data.

If you export a bill, report, or backup file, that file is created and saved on your device or shared through whichever app you choose (e.g. WhatsApp, email, a file manager). Bahi has no visibility into what happens to that file once it leaves the app.

4. Subscription and payment data

Bahi's subscription is sold and billed through your device's app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store). Payment details — card numbers, UPI IDs, billing addresses — are collected and processed entirely by Google or Apple under their own privacy policies. Bahi never sees or stores your payment information; it only receives a confirmation from the store that your subscription is active.

5. Permissions the app may request

Depending on the features you use, Bahi may ask for permissions such as storage/photos access (to attach a product image or export a PDF bill) or contacts access (to quickly add a shop or customer). Each permission is used only for the feature it's tied to, and only on your device — none of it is transmitted anywhere.

6. Children's privacy

Bahi is a business tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, let alone children, since the app does not collect information in the first place.

7. Changes to this policy

If Bahi ever adds a feature that changes how data is handled — for example, an optional cloud backup — this page will be updated first, and the "Last updated" date at the top will change accordingly.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to moinkhokhar2730vdr@gmail.com.

Section 03

Refunds & Cancellation

Refunds are handled by the app store, not by Bahi directly. Because subscriptions are billed through Google Play or the Apple App Store, all payment collection and refund processing is carried out by them under their own policies.

1. Cancelling your subscription

  • Android (Google Play): Open the Play Store → Profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bahi → Cancel.
  • iOS (App Store): Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Bahi → Cancel Subscription.

Cancelling stops future renewals; you'll keep access to paid features until the end of the current billing period.

2. Requesting a refund

Refund requests for charges are made directly with the store you subscribed through:

  • Google Play: support.google.com/googleplay
  • Apple App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com

Google and Apple set their own refund windows and eligibility rules, and their decision is final for store-processed payments.

3. When we can help directly

If you were charged incorrectly due to a bug in Bahi (for example, a duplicate purchase caused by an app error), write to moinkhokhar2730vdr@gmail.com with your order ID from the app store, and we'll help you pursue a resolution with the store or address the issue.

4. No partial refunds for unused time

Except where required by law or by the app store's own policy, we don't offer pro-rated refunds for the unused portion of a billing period after cancellation.

Section 04

Delete your data

Since Bahi stores everything locally and has no server, there's no "account" to delete on our end — deleting your data is something you do directly on your device.

1. Delete data but keep the app

If Bahi has an in-app option (Settings → Clear/Reset data), use that to wipe your bills, stock, and shop records while keeping the app installed.

2. Delete everything by removing the app

PlatformSteps
AndroidLong-press the Bahi icon → Uninstall, or Settings → Apps → Bahi → Uninstall. This removes the local database along with the app.
iOSLong-press the Bahi icon → Remove App → Delete App. This removes the app and its local storage.
DesktopUninstall Bahi through your OS's standard application manager, or delete the app's data folder if you'd like to remove local files without uninstalling.

3. Before you delete

Uninstalling or clearing app data is permanent and cannot be undone by us — we don't hold a copy to restore. If you might need your records later, export a backup or PDF of your bills/stock first, from within the app.

4. Your subscription is separate

Deleting the app does not cancel an active subscription. Cancel it through Google Play or the App Store (see the Refunds & Cancellation section above) to stop future billing.

5. Need help?

If you're unsure how to fully remove your data or have trouble with any of the above, email moinkhokhar2730vdr@gmail.com and we'll walk you through it.
